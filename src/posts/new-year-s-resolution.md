While I have a handful of personal [New Year's Resolutions](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Year's_resolution), I haven't yet chosen anything that is really technology-based. So, after thinking about it I've decided to **release a new application every week!**

It may be for Windows, Debian/Linux, Android, JavaScript+HTML5+CSS3, or something else!? It may or may not be open-source (ie: provided with source code), although most likely they will all be released under an [MIT License](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_License).

I've already uploaded a few applications to [github](https://github.com/kodybrown?tab=repositories), including something I wrote yesterday - [wifian](https://github.com/kodybrown/wifian), which is a small WiFi Signal Strength Analyzer..

So, here goes...
